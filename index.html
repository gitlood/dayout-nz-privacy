Day Out NZ – Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026

Day Out NZ helps you plan an outdoor day out in New Zealand: beach and water conditions, nearby walks, campsites and swim spots. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: we do not collect, store or share any personal data.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your device's location to sort nearby places by distance. Your location is processed only on your device. It is never sent to us or to any data provider, never stored on a server, and never logged. You can deny location permission and choose an area manually instead; the app works either way.

No accounts, analytics or ads

There are no user accounts. The app contains no analytics, tracking, crash-reporting or advertising software and does not use advertising identifiers. Your favourites and settings are saved only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Data the app downloads

To show conditions, the app downloads public information about places (not about you) from open-data providers: Open-Meteo (weather, UV and marine forecasts), LAWA (water quality), the Department of Conservation (walks, campsites, huts) and New Zealand regional and district councils (dog rules and local layers). Requests contain the coordinates of the places being looked up, not your location. Like any internet request, these providers receive your device's IP address as part of the connection; their own privacy policies apply to that.

Directions

If you tap Directions, the app opens Google Maps with the chosen destination. What happens in Google Maps is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Google Play

Google may collect information when you download or update the app through Google Play, as described in Google's privacy policy. We do not receive personal information from this.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
